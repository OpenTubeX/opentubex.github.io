  1. v0.37.4 Beta

    v0.37.4-betaLatestNov 6, 2026

    Highlights

    • A bell in the top bar keeps your live stream reminders, new uploads, and automatic downloads, which appear in the app instead of as system notifications while you use it. Settings can switch them to system only or off, or hide ones that only confirm an action. (#2587, #2646, #2606, #2618)
    • The app takes on Material 3 Expressive, with rounder corners, tinted buttons, springier motion, bolder titles, and smoothly animated menus and dialogs that blur the page behind them. Custom themes keep working. (#2662, #2691, #2703, #2771, #2753, #2758)

    New features

    • RSS feeds can now refresh automatically, with new items marked and shown in the New feed, and the feed manager can browse RSSHub to fill in feed addresses. (#2635, #2653, #2659, #2671)
    • The new Video descriptions setting in Focus settings and Quick Settings hides descriptions, keeps them collapsed, or opens them expanded. (#2502, #2651)
    • Delete Old History on the History page can now delete your entire watch history with the new “All time” option. (#2545, #2650)
    • Channels with TV shows now have a Shows tab, which you can hide in Focus settings. (#2729)
    • You can now pin and unpin channels from their context menus. (#2708)
    • Desktop: The Material accent color picker now offers your system accent color and follows it when you change it. (#2626)
    • Desktop: You can now set the height of vertical tabs in Appearance settings. (#2709)
    • The About page and the update prompt now link to the new donation page, where you can support OpenTubeX through GitHub Sponsors or Liberapay. (#2759)

    Improvements

    • OpenTubeX starts faster with a large watch history, long playlists open without loading every page first, and long queues and transcripts scroll smoothly. Editing large histories and playlists and handling large backups no longer slows down or freezes the app. (#2642, #2643, #2644)
    • The cast menu lists devices of every casting protocol together and, while casting, becomes a remote with seek and volume sliders, playback buttons, and subtitle choices. (#2682)
    • Videos from other sites now work more like YouTube videos, with transcripts and the same side panels, and their watch progress syncs between your devices. (#2639, #2637)
    • In the phone layout, the description now opens from a button like Comments, with a two-line excerpt and chips for its music, games, tags, and content labels. (#2667)
    • Long video and playlist titles now stop after two lines with an ellipsis, which you can turn off with Limit titles to two lines in Appearance settings. (#2602, #2622)
    • The Ocean themes are now Tally Light and Tally Dark, with a red accent color by default that you can change. (#2751)
    • Desktop: Videos in the watch page playlist have a one-tap Remove from playlist button on their thumbnails again. (#2603, #2620)
    • OpenTubeX no longer contacts api.invidious.io at startup unless it uses a random Invidious instance. (#2642)
    • macOS, Windows: OpenTubeX is now available through Scoop, and nightly builds through Scoop and Homebrew. The update prompt shows the matching update command for these installs.
    • macOS: OpenTubeX now requires macOS 13 or later, because Electron no longer supports macOS 12. (#2621)
    • Linux: 32-bit ARM (armv7l) builds are no longer available, because Electron no longer supports them. 64-bit ARM (arm64) builds are still available. (#2621)

    Fixed bugs

    • Videos keep their selected quality on slow or high-latency connections instead of dropping to 360p and reloading, and dropped frames now lower the resolution instead of switching to a harder-to-decode codec such as AV1. (#2604, #2664, #2701)
    • Videos and Shorts no longer take minutes to open while YouTube rate-limits your connection. (#2748)
    • Android, iOS: Tapping Next or Previous in the player controls switches to the next or previous video in a playlist or queue again. (#2608, #2614)
    • Desktop: Older completed, queued, and paused downloads no longer disappear from Downloads after a restart. (#2643)
    • A failed search history import no longer erases your existing search history. (#2642)
    • Linux, Windows: The app no longer crashes when you press a menu shortcut, such as Ctrl+, to open the settings, after watching a video. (#2669)
    • Android: Playback no longer crashes the app when the video server answers with a redirect or a not-modified response. (#2643)
    • Android, Desktop: Downloaded videos now start playing within a few seconds when YouTube can’t be reached, even while the rest of the internet works. (#2657)
    • When Start Videos Automatically is off, the next playlist or recommended video now starts after the countdown or Play Now, and looping a playlist with one video replays it every time. (#2768, #2773)
    • Failed or incomplete comment loads now offer a retry instead of reporting no comments or retrying over and over. (#2779)
    • Captions now grow and shrink with the player, so they cover the same share of the video in a window, in fullscreen, and in the mini player. (#2628, #2632)
    • Videos with captions in your preferred caption language now show those captions instead of the auto-translation you last picked. (#2733)
    • Android, Desktop: Background subscription refresh no longer reports errors for channels without Shorts, live streams, or posts, or mistakes their regular uploads for Shorts or live streams. (#2789)
