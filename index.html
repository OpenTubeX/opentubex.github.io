Why OpenTubeX

Five reasons to switch

A client that stays private, flexible, and yours to shape.

01

End-to-end encrypted sync

Settings and watch state travel encrypted between your machines. Only you hold the keys. The sync stack is open source and self-hostable if you want your own server.

02

Private by default

OpenTubeX does not profile you. No analytics, no fingerprinting, no silent telemetry.

03

Feature-rich

Tabs, SponsorBlock, Auto PiP, transcripts, downloads, Ambient Mode, and more.

04

Built to customize

Themes, shortcuts, per-channel quality and speed, layout tweaks, and deep settings let you shape the client around how you actually watch.

05

Open source, auditable

Inspect the code, fork it, and verify the claims for yourself.
