## Find a video or channel

1. Enter a title, channel name, or YouTube link in the search field.
2. Submit the search and open a result.
3. Open the channel name to browse more uploads from that creator.

OpenTubeX keeps your subscriptions and local playlists in the app. You do not need to sign in to YouTube, and these changes do not update your YouTube account.

## Subscribe to a channel

1. Open the channel you want to follow.
2. Select **Subscribe**.
3. Open **Subscriptions** in the navigation to see uploads from your channels. On a small screen, some navigation entries are under **More**.
4. Use the feed's refresh action to fetch new uploads.

Already have subscriptions elsewhere? [Import your existing subscriptions](/docs/importing/#import-subscriptions) instead of adding channels one by one.

## Use profiles

Profiles let you group channels, for example into work and leisure viewing.

1. Open the profile menu from your profile icon.
2. Use the profile selector to choose or manage a profile.
3. Check which profile is active when viewing your subscriptions.

If a channel appears to be missing, check **All Channels** before importing it again. Feed filters can also hide videos, Shorts, or live content.

For step-by-step profile creation and bulk channel management, see [Organize channels with profiles](/docs/profiles/).

## Create a local playlist

1. Open **Playlists** in the navigation.
2. Select **Create New Playlist**.
3. Enter a name and select **Create**.
4. On a video, use **Add to Playlist** and choose the playlist. On desktop, video cards also expose actions on hover; use the video's menu when an action is not visible.
5. Return to **Playlists** and open your playlist to watch its videos.

## Open settings

1. Select your profile icon to open the quick settings menu.
2. Choose **All settings**.
3. Select a category or use settings search to find an option by name.
