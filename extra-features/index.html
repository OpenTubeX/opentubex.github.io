60 extras worth knowing

Screenshots and details for each feature.

Per-channel playback speed

Remember playback speed on a per-channel basis. You can enable it in settings, save automatically when you change speed, or use a button under the player to save manually.

Per-channel video quality

Remember video quality on a per-channel basis with the same workflow as per-channel playback speed.

Optional seek-interval behavior

Choose whether seek intervals (arrow keys and J/L) are multiplied by playback rate.

Playback-rate adjusted timestamps

Optionally show timestamps adjusted for the current playback rate.

Chapter tooltips and chapter sidebar

Display improved chapter tooltips directly on the seekbar and show the current chapter list in the sidebar.

Transcript panel

Follow a video’s transcript in a dedicated panel.

Caption appearance and preferred language

Customize caption appearance and choose a preferred caption language.

Prevent YouTube translations

Option to prevent title and description translations from YouTube.

Quick search focus

Focus the search bar by pressing /, in addition to Ctrl+L or Cmd+L.

In-page search

Find text on the current page with Ctrl+F or Cmd+F, including previous and next match navigation.

SponsorBlock side panel

Open a side panel with segment details, a temporary auto-skip toggle, and an option to whitelist the current channel.

SponsorBlock tooltips (unskip / reskip / prompt to skip)

Improved SponsorBlock tooltips with unskip, reskip, and prompt-to-skip when you need to replay a segment, skip it again, or decide on a segment before it is skipped.

SponsorBlock enhancements

Display SponsorBlock community-contributed chapters, including Highlight and Hook/Greetings segments, in the player. Hover over a seekbar segment to see its category label.

SponsorBlock submission

Submit SponsorBlock segments directly from the player. This feature is experimental.

Tab support

Use browser-style tabs in horizontal or vertical layouts, with pinning, colors, thumbnail previews, duplication, unloading, reordering, moving between windows, bulk closing, YouTube link copying, and configurable session restoration.

IP block recovery script

Set a script to run when YouTube blocks your IP. OpenTubeX waits for the script to finish, then reloads the video.

Auto Picture-in-Picture

Automatically enter Picture-in-Picture when you switch tabs, minimize the app, or switch windows.

Scroll mini-player

Keep watching in a mini-player when you scroll away from a video.

YouTube end-screen annotations

Show YouTube end-screen annotations inside the player.

Ambient Mode

Add an ambient glow around the player based on the current video.

Loop & Copy link in player context menu

Quickly toggle looping or copy the video link from the player’s context menu.

Quick Playback Speed Bar

Add an optional, fully customizable quick playback speed bar to the player.

Fast-forward through silence

Optionally speed through silent parts of a video. Enable this behavior in Player settings.

Temporary 2× playback

Hold the left mouse button or Space to temporarily double the playback speed.

Return YouTube Dislike support

Show dislike count using Return YouTube Dislike.

Remember volume

Remember the player volume between sessions. Enabled by default.

Sleep timer

Stop playback after a chosen duration or when the current video ends.

Playback speed toggle shortcut

Press G to toggle between 1× playback speed and your last used speed.

Auto-refresh subscriptions

Automatically refresh subscriptions on a schedule so your feed stays current without a manual refresh.

History retention and cleanup

Automatically clean up watch history after a configurable retention period, or manually delete older entries.

Watch time statistics

Explore your viewing activity with daily and weekly watch-time charts.

Configurable thumbnail action

Choose an extra action button to show on video thumbnails.

Configurable thumbnail sizes

Choose the thumbnail size used while browsing videos.

Additional video metadata

See more context about a video, including AI-generated content labels, collaborators, categories, relative publication dates, and comment counts.

Playlist playback controls

Reorder or remove playlist items during playback and remember the reverse state of each playlist.

Comment tools

Render nested reply threads, show when comments were edited, reload comments, or copy a comment’s direct YouTube link.

LibreTube import

Import subscriptions and watch history from LibreTube.

Close confirmation

Optionally ask for confirmation before closing the app.

Download support

Download videos directly via yt-dlp.

Full-screen docks

Open comments, playlists, or chapters in full-screen docks.

Customizable keyboard shortcuts

Choose the keyboard shortcuts used throughout the app.

Rewind live streams and premieres

Experimentally rewind live streams and premieres instead of being limited to the live edge.

New subscription content

Show indicators for newly fetched content and optionally collect it in a separate new-content feed.

Autoplay preview

See the next video and its countdown directly on the player before autoplay starts it.

Animation controls

Use the app’s UI animations or force-enable or disable them independently of your system setting.

Premiere indicators

Distinguish premieres from live streams in video lists and metadata.

Resizable playlist card

Make the playlist card taller or shorter while watching a video.

Live chat channel links

Open a chatter’s channel by clicking their handle in live chat.

Encrypted sync

Synchronize subscriptions, playlists, history, per-channel playback speeds, profiles, tabs, and settings with end-to-end encryption. LibreTube Sync servers are supported too.

Watch queue

Queue videos from their three-dot menus, then manage and drag to reorder them in a side panel on the watch page.

Inline add-to-playlist

Choose playlists from an inline popover, and pick an icon for the quick bookmark playlist.

Selection web search

Search selected text using your chosen search engine.

Search history and channel filters

Revisit searches with their filters restored, and use filter chips when searching within a channel.

Watched-percentage threshold

Choose how much of a video must play before it counts as watched. Set the threshold to 0% to mark videos as watched immediately.

Changed-settings highlights

Highlight settings that differ from their defaults and reset them directly.

Toast notifications

Customize the position of toast notifications with icons, optional timeout indicators, and video thumbnails. Dismiss them by swiping or pressing Escape.

Interface rounding and overlay scrollbars

Adjust how rounded interface elements are and choose whether overlay scrollbars automatically hide or remain visible.

YouTube-style Shorts

Choose between a YouTube-style Shorts layout and player or the regular video grid and player.

SponsorBlock full-video labels and mute segments

Display and submit SponsorBlock full-video labels and mute segments.

Subscription feed refresh

Cancel subscription refreshes and see new videos immediately while the rest loads.

Take the features with you

Grab a signed build or install through the package manager you already use.
